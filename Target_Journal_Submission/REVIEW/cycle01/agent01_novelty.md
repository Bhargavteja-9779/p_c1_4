# Reviewer #1 (Novelty & prior art), cycle 1

## Summary of the submission (3-5 sentences)
The manuscript converts a stager's epoch probabilities into per-night 90 % intervals for eight PSG parameters: temperature scaling, a tempered hybrid HMM sampled by FFBS, then split-conformal calibration of posterior mean ± SD. It is evaluated with YASA and an in-domain LightGBM on Sleep-EDF SC/ST and HMC (348 nights) against Bland–Altman and distribution-free LoA, CQR, entropy scaling and ablations, including cross-cohort transfer and 10–50-night recalibration. Claimed novelty: (a) per-night distribution-free intervals for PSG parameters; (b) structured posterior hypnogram sampling as conformal scale; (c) cross-cohort coverage and few-night recalibration; (d) the centre × scale factorial (Intro ¶3–5; Discussion; supplementary literature review "Not found" 1–3).

## Fatal concerns
None; the surviving contribution is publishable if repositioned.

## Critical/Major concerns
**M1 (critical): the closest prior art is uncited and the stated gap is false.** Intro ¶3: "Uncertainty quantification for sleep staging has so far been developed at the epoch level"; the Discussion contrasts FFBS only with independent sampling and Hardarson. P1/P2 already propagate sampled whole hypnograms to per-recording TST/SOL/REM-latency distributions and show, like this paper's ablation (Results 3.3), that joint beats factorized sampling. *Action:* cite P1/P2; credit posterior sampling as prior work in Intro ¶3 and "Relation to previous work"; present the FFBS ablation as confirmation with a cheap model-agnostic sampler; state the true differences: (i) coverage guarantee against the clinical reference (P1/P2 target inter-scorer spread, no coverage); (ii) no stager retraining.

**M2 (major): patent prior art covers the sampling-to-flagging workflow, and Highlight 1 overclaims.** P3 claims sampled-hypnogram uncertainty of TST, SOL, WASO, SE, AHI thresholded to flag nights; Highlight 1 ("Automatic sleep scoring gives no per-night error range…") and Intro ¶2 imply none exists. *Action:* cite P3 (or P1) in "Clinical use"/Section 3.8; reword Highlight 1 (e.g. "…is validated only with population limits of agreement").

**M3 (major): the novelty must be repositioned on what the data actually show.** With (b) credited to P1–P3, the distinctive evidence is: (i) uncalibrated posterior intervals covered only 27–86 % (independent sampling as low as 4 %; Results 3.2), i.e. P1/P3-style spreads need conformal calibration; (ii) cross-cohort N3 % collapse (38 %) and 10-night recovery (91 %). Gains over the clinical standard are mixed: better in 9, worse in 4 of 24 combinations versus distribution-free LoA (Table 2); the point-estimate gain comes from recalibration, not sampling (Table 3). *Action:* make (i)–(ii) the primary contributions (Abstract, Intro ¶5, Discussion ¶1); demote "FFBS scale"; show comparator (g) as the "uncalibrated posterior sampling (P1/P3)" baseline in Table 2 or Fig. 2.

## Moderate concerns
**Mo1. Vainikka 2026 is uncited and model-native sampling is missing as a scale.** `vainikka2026` and `kaneda2026` are in references.json but never cited, although the authors' review says Vainikka "must be cited"; "Hypnodensity Interval" invites confusion. MC-dropout/ensemble disagreement (P6; SOMNUS) is the natural competitor to the HMM scale. *Action:* cite and contrast; ideally add a pretrained deep stager/ensemble (SLEEPYLAND, U-Sleep) and compare FFBS vs native-sampling scales (also addresses limitation iv).

**Mo2. Kang 2021 and van Gorp 2022 are mischaracterized.** Kang is listed under "Entropy" though its stager is an HMM with forward–backward posteriors (P5), the nearest antecedent of Eq. 3; van Gorp 2022, an uncertainty framework raising sleep-parameter error, is cited only for hypnodensity display. *Action:* describe accurately.

**Mo3. A claim is attributed to Hardarson that the abstract does not support.** Intro ¶2 attributes "because epoch errors are temporally clustered" to Hardarson, whose abstract describes Monte Carlo simulation from published error statistics without temporal clustering. *Action:* verify in full text or attribute to P2.

**Mo4. Claim (c) is presented without its antecedents.** Few-recording local recalibration exists for staging (Ganglberger 2024: 324 local cases) and conformal EEG (P7, P11). *Action:* cite in Section 3.5/"Clinical use"; frame as quantifying how many nights restore coverage.

## Minor concerns
**Mi1.** Concurrent work (P4, P7) should be acknowledged if it is published before revision. The internal comparison document and the cover letter should avoid "first" claims (REVIEW/comparison_with_strongest_journal_papers.md row "Novelty": "First per-night conformal intervals…; FFBS-based adaptive scale; factorial separation").
**Mi2.** Normalized conformal with an external difficulty scale is textbook (Lei 2018, locally weighted), and conformal intervals for biosignal-derived targets exist (P11). The centre × scale factorial (d) should be described as an analysis design, not a contribution.
**Mi3.** The stage-percentage intervals are marginal and per parameter, and they ignore the compositional constraint and joint coverage across the eight parameters. Cite P10 and state this as a limitation.
**Mi4.** The supplementary literature review's "Not found" item 2 is wrong, and Table B omits P1–P3. Correct both, and add IEEE Xplore to the searches: P1 and P2 are IEEE.

## Required additional experiments/analyses
1. (Text) Rewrite Intro ¶2–3, Discussion "Relation to previous work", Highlight 1 and the cover letter as in M1–M3. Add P1, P2, Vainikka 2026 and P3 (or P1 only); this stays within the 50-reference limit (41 are cited now).
2. Relabel comparator (g) as the "uncalibrated posterior sampling" baseline representing P1/P3. Report its coverage and interval score for all cohorts and both stagers in a main display item.
3. (Recommended) Add one deep stager with native sampling (MC dropout or ensemble, e.g. SLEEPYLAND SOMNUS/U-Sleep). Compare an FFBS scale, a native-sampling scale and an entropy scale under the same conformal wrapper.
4. (Optional) On public multi-scored data (DOD-H/DOD-O), report coverage against each scorer and against the consensus. This separates the paper's target (error relative to a reference) from P1's target (inter-rater spread).

## Recommendation
**Major revision.** The central conceptual ingredient (b), whole-hypnogram posterior sampling propagated to night-level sleep statistics with a joint-versus-independent comparison, was published in 2023 (IEEE JBHI; ICASSP) and patented by Philips. These works are uncited, and the Introduction states a gap that does not exist. What remains, conformally calibrated per-night intervals in clinical units with a coverage guarantee, empirical failure of transport and few-night recalibration, against the Bland–Altman standard, was not found in any peer-reviewed source and is relevant to *Sleep Medicine*. It is, however, incremental methodologically, and its advantage over distribution-free LoA is parameter-specific. Acceptance requires honest repositioning (M1–M3) and correct characterization of the cited works.
